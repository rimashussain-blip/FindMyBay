Why findMy Bay

Built around your time, not the queue.

We started findMy Bay because waiting for a wash on a 38° afternoon shouldn't be normal. Every studio on the platform is vetted, every bay is reserved in advance, and every receipt earns loyalty.

  • No more waiting around
    Reserve a bay before you arrive — the studio is ready when you are.
  • Vetted, rated studios
    We visit, audit, and rate every partner. Below 4.5★ means they're off the platform.
  • One price, no surprises
    What you see at booking is what you pay. VAT included, refunds always honoured.
  • Loyalty that adds up
    Earn a star with every wash. Five stars unlocks a free Standard Wash at any partner.
